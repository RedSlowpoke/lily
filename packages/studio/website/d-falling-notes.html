Lily Studio GitHub

Write the score. Watch it play.

Lily Studio engraves your LilyPond score as you type, then plays it back, part by part, or onto a falling keyboard like this one.

Open in your browser Download for Mac

Free. The Mac app is for macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

In your browser

Sign in and your scores are kept in My Scores, engraved on our server. Upload and download them from the menu bar.

You need a browser. Nothing to install.

Open Lily Studio

On your Mac

The same studio with your scores in your own folders, plus Stems: split a song into vocals, drums, bass and the rest, and start a score from any of them.

You need macOS 14 and LilyPond; the app walks you through setting it up. Stems needs Apple Silicon.

Mistakes in plain words

Every part, your way

Mute a part while it plays, or give it another instrument.

PDF and MIDI
