Write music, not code

  • Engraved as you typeThe score beside the text follows each keystroke.
  • Mistakes in plain words“\stacato” is not a LilyPond command, and a click takes you to it.
  • Hear itNotes light up as they play. Mute a part, or give it another instrument.
  • Piano viewThe notes fall onto a keyboard, as in piano videos.
  • Print it, share itExport the PDF, or the MIDI with or without its muted parts.
  • Stems, on the MacMake a karaoke track, or separate a song’s instruments and transcribe one.
