Lily Studio

A small studio for LilyPond scores

Free and open source On your Mac, or in your browser

In your browser

Nothing to install

Sign in and start writing. Your scores are kept in My Scores, engraved as you type, and you can upload and download them from the menu bar.

Open Lily Studio

On your Mac

The whole studio, with Stems

Everything the web has, plus Stems: split a song into vocals, drums, bass and the rest, and start a score from any of them. It uses LilyPond on your Mac; the app shows you how to set it up.

Download for Mac Intel Mac

Version 0.1.0. Signed and notarized by Apple. Stems needs Apple Silicon.

Performance notes

Engraved as you type
The score follows the text, a keystroke behind.
Mistakes in plain words
“\stacato” is not a LilyPond command, and a click takes you there.
Click a note
It finds that note in the text.
Play it back
Notes light up as they sound. Mute a part, or give it another instrument.
Piano view
Notes fall onto a keyboard, as in piano videos.
PDF and MIDI
Export the printable score, or the music with or without its muted parts.

Music engraving by LilyPond, www.lilypond.org
