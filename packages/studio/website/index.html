Lily Studio site options

Four drafts of the same page: open the studio in the browser, or download it for the Mac.

  1. A. The title page

    The page is set like the first page of a score: dedication, title, composer, then a staff of Ode to Joy whose words describe the app, and which plays when asked.

  2. B. The window

    The studio itself as the hero: notes are typed and engraved one by one, a misspelt command is explained in plain words and fixed. A table says what runs where.

  3. C. Two doors

    The page is the choice: the screen splits into the browser and the Mac, each half one big link, with the lily on the seam.

  4. D. Falling notes

    The Piano view as the hero: Ode to Joy falls onto a keyboard, right hand in violet, left in gold, with sound on request.
