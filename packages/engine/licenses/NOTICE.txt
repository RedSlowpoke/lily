Lily's engine: what this directory serves, and under which licences.

The engine (core.wasm, core-debug.wasm, the split .wasm modules, pdf.wasm,
core.image and the .part files) is free software under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 or later (LICENSE.txt). It ports GNU LilyPond 2.26.0's C++ and
carries LilyPond's Scheme and ly/ files in its image. Its source is the
repository and commit that manifest.json names under "source".

The music font packs (emmentaler-*.pack) are LilyPond's Emmentaler and
braces, as LilyPond 2.26.0 ships them in fonts/otf, in another encoding:
under the GPL with the font exception, or the SIL Open Font License, as
LilyPond's own LICENSE says (LILYPOND-LICENSE.txt, LILYPOND-LICENSE.OFL.txt).

The text packs (C059-*, NimbusSans-*, NimbusMonoPS-*, each a .pack and a
.outlines.pack) are URW's base 35 fonts, as LilyPond 2.26.0 ships them in
fonts/otf, in another encoding: under the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 (URW-COPYING.txt), with URW's exception for PostScript and PDF
documents (URW-LICENSE.txt). Their source is
https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts and LilyPond 2.26.0's
fonts/otf; the tool that encodes them is packages/engine/tools/packs/text.mjs
of the engine's source.

The TeX Gyre Schola packs (TeXGyreSchola-*, each a .pack and a .outlines.pack),
which stand for "New Century Schoolbook" as LilyPond's fontconfig gives it, are
TeX Gyre Schola 2.005, as LilyPond 2.26.0 ships it in fonts/otf, in another
encoding: "Copyright 2006, 2009 for TeX Gyre extensions by B. Jackowski and
J.M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups)", under the GUST Font License
(TEX-GYRE-LICENSE.txt), an instance of the LaTeX Project Public License,
version 1.3c (LPPL-1.3c.txt). Their source is
https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre and LilyPond 2.26.0's fonts/otf.

The OTFs beside them (C059-*.otf, NimbusSans-*.otf, NimbusMonoPS-*.otf,
TeXGyreSchola-*.otf) are those fonts as LilyPond 2.26.0 ships them,
unmodified, under the same licences, each renamed to its PostScript name:
the page draws a page's text in them, and the PDF module embeds subsets of
them.

The SVG pages the engine writes draw Emmentaler's glyphs as paths, which
its font exception covers, and name the text fonts without embedding them.
The PDFs its PDF module (pdf.wasm) writes draw Emmentaler's glyphs as
outlines, which its font exception covers, and embed subsets of the URW
fonts, their programs unmodified but for the glyphs left out, which URW's
exception for PostScript and PDF documents covers, and of TeX Gyre Schola,
which the GUST Font License allows.
